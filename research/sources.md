# Community-Led Growth for Recruiting and Professional Ecosystems

## Research Thesis

This research explores how Community-Led Growth can help recruiting platforms, ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems), HR Tech companies, and professional ecosystems build trust, engagement, advocacy, and sustainable growth beyond traditional acquisition channels.

The objective is to identify high-signal experts whose work helps explain how communities create value, strengthen professional relationships, increase retention, and support long-term business growth.

Rather than focusing exclusively on acquisition tactics, this research examines how community participation, professional trust, knowledge sharing, and user advocacy can become strategic growth mechanisms.
