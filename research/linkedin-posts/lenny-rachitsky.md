Summary:

Lenny summarizes Benedict Evans' views on AI, technological disruption, distribution, and long-term market evolution.

Key Insights:

- AI remains in an early adoption phase.
- Technological disruption creates both opportunities and risks.
- Distribution is becoming increasingly valuable as software becomes easier to build.
- AI infrastructure may become commoditized over time.
- Value creation will likely shift toward applications and distribution layers.

Community-Led Growth Relevance:

Communities can become a powerful distribution advantage. As technology becomes easier to replicate, trust, audience relationships, and community engagement become increasingly difficult to copy.
