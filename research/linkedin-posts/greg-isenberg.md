The Rise of Agent-Native Businesses

Date:

2026-06-02

Link:

[https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gisenberg_theres-probably-100-billion-up-for-grabs-activity-7467657776874397696-uBFV](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gisenberg_theres-probably-100-billion-up-for-grabs-activity-7467657776874397696-uBFV)

Summary:

Greg argues that AI agents will become a major class of digital customers. He suggests entrepreneurs should begin building products and services specifically designed for AI agents rather than human users.

Key Insights:

- Future digital commerce may increasingly occur between agents rather than humans.
- Traditional user acquisition funnels may become obsolete for agent-native products.
- APIs (Application Programming Interfaces), MCPs (Model Context Protocols), and machine-readable systems become critical infrastructure.
- Entire software categories may be rebuilt specifically for autonomous agents.

Community-Led Growth Relevance:

Communities often identify emerging technology shifts before mainstream adoption. Understanding how communities discuss and adopt AI tools may provide early signals for future business opportunities.
