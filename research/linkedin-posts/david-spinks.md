### #### Post 3

Topic:

The Community Commitment Curve

Date:

2026-06-04

Link:

[https://www.linkedin.com/posts/davidspinks_building-a-community-business-101-day-5-activity-7468650352410603520-vbZr?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADaOEQsBQxJCUsb_KGK8ohQumm2Xy2nrXkU](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/davidspinks_building-a-community-business-101-day-5-activity-7468650352410603520-vbZr?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADaOEQsBQxJCUsb_KGK8ohQumm2Xy2nrXkU)

Summary:

David Spinks explains the concept of the Community Commitment Curve, originally learned from Douglas Atkin (former community leader at Meetup and Airbnb). The framework helps community builders understand how members gradually increase their level of participation and commitment over time.

Key Insights:

- Members should not be asked for high levels of commitment too early.

- Community participation develops progressively through small actions and experiences.

- Intentional pathways can guide members from passive consumption to active leadership.

- Community growth depends on creating clear next steps for deeper engagement.

- Some members naturally increase commitment when presented with the right invitation.

Framework Mentioned:

Community Commitment Curve

Examples of progression:

- Read content

- Attend events

- Join discussions

- Help other members

- Mentor newcomers

- Lead initiatives

- Assume leadership roles

Community-Led Growth Relevance:

This framework provides a practical model for designing member journeys. Rather than focusing solely on acquisition, Community-Led Growth emphasizes helping members gradually deepen their participation, contribution, and ownership within the ecosystem.

## Strategic Relevance

David Spinks provides foundational frameworks for understanding how professional communities become sustainable growth engines.

His work is particularly relevant for recruiting and talent-focused platforms because it explains how trust, participation, and member ownership can generate long-term engagement and network effects.

The Community Commitment Curve offers a practical model for guiding users from passive participation to active contribution, a principle that can be directly applied to community-led growth initiatives within recruiting ecosystems.
